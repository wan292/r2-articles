# Three-dimensional mandibular regional superimposition in growing patients (Nguyen 2018)

Nguyen T, Cevidanes L, Franchi L, Ruellas A, Jackson T. Am J Orthod Dentofacial Orthop 2018;153(5):747-754. DOI 10.1016/j.ajodo.2017.07.026. Techno Bytes (technique report). UNC Chapel Hill, Michigan, Florence, Rio de Janeiro. IRB 12-1496; NIDCR and AAOF funded; no conflicts.

## Bottom line

Serial CBCT mandibles registered on the anterior chin plus the inner symphyseal cortex reproduce a bone-plate reference to about one voxel (0.37 and 0.40 mm), while the third-molar crypt floor misses by 1.94 mm (Table). Two observers using the combined chin + symphysis field agreed to 0.12 ± 0.11 mm (ICC 0.998). Björk's chin and symphysis survive in 3D; his crypt and canal do not.

## What they did

- Aim 1, stability: 20 Class III bone-anchor patients (T1 12.1 ± 1.3 y, T2 13.3 ± 1.1 y). Their plates and screws at the lateral-incisor/canine apices served as regional implants. T2 was registered to T1 on the plates (Slicer CMF voxel registration; 0.3 mm CBCT voxels) and the absolute mean surface distance was measured at the chin (pogonion to B point), the symphysis (lingual cortex up to the mental foramina) and the third-molar crypt floors. The canal was excluded a priori.
- Aim 2, reliability: 25 growing patients (12.7 ± 1.4 y, CVM 2-5, 12.6 ± 0.9 months; 10 Herbst, 10 Class II elastics, 5 Class III anchors). Two observers independently registered T2 to T1 on chin + symphysis.

## What they found

- Chin 0.37 ± 0.16 mm (P = .765); symphysis 0.40 ± 0.14 mm (P = .698; text prints 0.15); third-molar crypt 1.94 ± 0.06 mm (P = .0025, test not named) (Table, p. 749).
- Interobserver error 0.12 ± 0.11 mm (Results; the abstract misprints ± 1.1); ICC 0.998 (95 % CI 0.995-1.000). One illustrated subject: whole mandible within 0.25 mm, 75 % of it within 0.12 mm (Fig 8).
- On that registration: pogonion unchanged, about 2 mm deposition near menton (Fig 4D), anterior ramus resorption and posterior deposition (Fig 6).

## Why it matters

It is the mandibular counterpart of Doppel's and Nielsen's maxillary implant work: the reference region, not the software, decides what "tooth movement" means. For the exam, Björk's four 2D structures (chin above pogonion, inner symphyseal cortex, canal-related trabeculae, third-molar germ before roots) reduce to two in voxel work. Ricketts' corpus axis and any lower-border reference run through bone this paper shows remodelling.

## One-line caveat

The reference is a loaded Class III bone plate judged immobile by hand, millimetres from the regions it validates, over about one year, in treated adolescents only, with no named test behind the Table's P values.

## Method at a glance

| Method | What is registered | What it gets right | What it gets wrong | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bone plates and screws (reference) | Plate volumes at the incisor apices | Metal does not remodel | Immobility checked clinically only; loaded by elastics; next to the chin | Methods |
| Chin + symphysis voxel registration (recommended) | Anterior chin pogonion to B point, plus lingual symphyseal cortex to the mental foramina | 0.37 / 0.40 mm vs plates; 0.12 mm between observers | Untested beyond 12.6 months or outside treated adolescents | Table; Results |
| Third-molar crypt floor | Inferior contour of the germ | Nothing | 1.94 mm error; said to be worse in vertical growers, with no data | Table; Fig 5 |
| Mandibular canal | Canal-related trabeculae | Not tested | Excluded: changes with growth | Methods; Björk 1963, Krarup 2005 (as cited) |
| Whole-mandible registration (Koerich 2016) | Entire mandibular volume | Non-growing patients | Folds growth into the fit in growers | Introduction (as cited) |
