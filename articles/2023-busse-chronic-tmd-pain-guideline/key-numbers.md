Category sources: the abstract gives the four lists. NSAIDs alone appear only in the infographic ("NSAIDs with or without steroids", conditional against); the abstract's list names NSAIDs only inside combinations.

Other splint numbers (Yao Fig 3):
- Removable splint, physical function: MD 11.03 (7.23 to 14.83), RD 32 % (24 to 40), L.
- Removable splint, adverse events: OR 1.34 (0.37 to 4.83), VL.
- Irreversible splint, physical function: MD 15.91 (7.41 to 24.42), RD 42 % (24 to 56), VL.
- NSAIDs + opioid, adverse events: OR 2.20 (0.04 to 123.00), VL.

Splint subgroup effects (Yao, Additional analysis): in removable splint vs placebo, high risk-of-bias trials gave larger effects. In removable splint vs usual care, longer follow-up gave smaller effects. Both had moderate credibility.

Physical function, moderate certainty (Yao, Physical functioning): RD for reaching a 5-point gain was 43 % (33 to 51) for jaw exercise + stretching, 43 % (25 to 56) for manipulation, 42 % (33 to 50) for acupuncture, and 36 % (19 to 51) for jaw exercise + mobilisation.
