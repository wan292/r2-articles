# Maxillary superimposition: a comparison of three methods for cephalometric evaluation of growth and treatment change (Nielsen, AJODO 1989)

Nielsen IL. Am J Orthod Dentofacial Orthop. 1989;95(5):422-431. Department of Growth and Development, University of California, San Francisco. Single author. No DOI is printed in the paper (pre-DOI issue; not looked up).

## Bottom line

In 18 implanted children followed 4 years, a palatal best fit registered at ANS hid 28-59 % of the vertical movement of the upper molars and incisors seen on implants (derived from Tables II and IV; abstract: 30-50 %), because it rides down with the resorbing nasal floor. The Björk-Skieller structural method (anterior surface of the zygomatic process) matched implants vertically within 0.33 mm but sat about 0.5 mm off horizontally. It is the implant evidence behind the structural superimposition the ABO requires.

## What they did

- Retrospective method comparison, 18 of 35 UCSF implant subjects (6 boys, 12 girls; T1 10 ± 0.37 y; interval 4 ± 0.56 y), selected for film quality and at least 3-4 stable zygomatic implants; treated and untreated pooled, mostly Class II, no Class III.
- Each film pair superimposed on (1) implants, (2) a palatal best fit registered at ANS, (3) the structural method with orbital-floor apposition set equal to nasal-floor resorption; implants left off the anatomic tracings.
- Displacement of PNS, ANS, ANS-Harvold, A point, U6 cusp and apex, U1 edge and apex in a Downs occlusal-plane grid fixed to T1; double determinations; a second observer for the structural method only.

## What they found

- Implants: ANS down 1.62 mm, PNS 0.70, A point 2.33, U6C 4.75, UIE 3.39 (Tables I-II).
- Best fit showed PNS and ANS moving slightly *up* (+0.36, +0.28 mm) and under-read every vertical change: implant minus best fit PNS 1.08, ANS 1.88, A point 2.24; U6C 1.33, U6A 1.46, UIE 1.84, UIA 1.71 mm, all P < .001 (Tables III-IV); PNS moved back 1.18 mm instead of 2.34 mm, half the growth in length.
- Structural vs implant: vertical −0.04 to −0.33 mm (NS); horizontal 0.53-0.66 mm, P < .01, at 7 of 8 landmarks.
- Observers differed vertically at ANS (0.88 mm), A point (0.77) and the incisor (0.52-0.86), not horizontally.

## Why it matters

A palatal best fit cannot see the nasal floor resorb, so in a growing patient it subtracts part of the eruption and palatal descent from every upper-tooth measurement, always in the same direction; Khaleel 2026 in this hub used a "palatal vault (Broadbent)" superimposition (registry). For the exam: the 3 : 2 apposition-to-resorption ratio, the structures the structural method uses, and why PTM, PNS and the infratemporal fossa fail (the maxilla lengthens at PNS; the posterior zygomatic surface is appositional).

## One-line caveat

Eighteen quality-selected films at the pubertal peak, an unnamed test, 64 uncorrected comparisons, no method error reported despite duplicate tracings, treated and untreated pooled, and implant rows in Tables III-IV that do not rebuild from Tables I-II (gaps up to 0.38 mm at A point), so n = 18 may not hold for every implant comparison.

## Method at a glance

| Method | What is registered | What it gets right | What it gets wrong | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implant (Björk) | At least 3-4 tantalum implants in the zygomatic process; inter-implant distance bisected; unstable implants dropped | Reference standard: sees nasal-floor resorption and sutural lengthening | Not clinically available; unilateral implants leave head-position error (the paper's own caveat) | Tables I-II |
| Best fit (Broadbent 1937; Ricketts) | Hard palate with the nasal floors aligned, registered at ANS and the ANS-A region | Direction of horizontal tooth change and growth in length "similar" to the other methods (Discussion) | Vertical under-read 1.08-2.24 mm skeletal and 1.33-1.84 mm dental per 4 y; every landmark 1.1-1.7 mm too anterior; PNS growth halved; ANS and PNS appear to rise | Tables III-IV, Fig 8 |
| Structural (Björk & Skieller 1977) | Anterior surface of both zygomatic processes, with orbital floors and palatal floor traced; T2 placed so orbital apposition equals nasal-floor resorption | Vertical change within 0.33 mm of implants (NS) | About 0.5 mm horizontal offset (P < .01); 0.8-0.9 mm interobserver bias at ANS and the incisor; needs clear bilateral zygomatic contours (only 18 of 35 films usable); a short process can rotate the fit | Tables III-IV, Fig 9 |
