# Key numbers — Nielsen 1989 (best fit vs implant vs structural maxillary superimposition)

Nielsen IL. Maxillary superimposition: a comparison of three methods for cephalometric evaluation of growth and treatment change. Am J Orthod Dentofacial Orthop 1989;95(5):422-431. Every cell of Tables I-IV below was read from the page images (journal pages 426-428) and checked twice against the extracted text. Units are mm unless stated. "Derived" = computed here from the printed numbers; the paired-t recomputations assume the printed SD is the SD of the within-subject differences with n = 18 (df = 17), and the arithmetic is in `checks.py` beside this file.

**Coordinate convention (Fig 6, p 426):** X axis = Downs occlusal plane (the line bisecting the occlusion of the first molars and central incisors); Y axis = perpendicular through the mesial cusps of the first molars; origin at U6C on the T1 film. +X = anterior (toward ANS and UIE), +Y = superior. A displacement is the T2 minus T1 position of a landmark after the T2 tracing has been superimposed by the method named. Negative Y = downward (descent, eruption); negative X = posterior. The author reads PNS X −2.34 as 2.3 mm of posterior displacement (p 427), which confirms the convention.

## Method / protocol

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Methods compared | (1) Implant; (2) best fit: optimal fit of the hard palate with nasal floors aligned, registered at ANS and the ANS-A region; (3) structural (Björk & Skieller): tracings of both zygomatic processes, orbital floors and palatal floor superimposed on the anterior surface of the zygomatic process, T2 oriented so orbital-floor apposition equals nasal-floor resorption |
| Implant registration | Implants traced, inter-implant distance bisected; any implant judged unstable excluded (criterion not stated) |
| Tracing | Separate mat-acetate tracing per film per method (Baumrind, Miller & Molthen technique); implants omitted from the best-fit and structural tracings to avoid bias |
| Determinations | Double, all three methods, separate occasions; which determination was analysed is not stated |
| Second observer | Structural method only, twice, "after brief instructions" |
| Coordinate system | Downs occlusal plane of the T1 film as X; perpendicular through the first-molar mesial cusps as Y (Fig 6) |
| Analysis | UCSF computer-aided head-film analysis (Baumrind & Miller 1980); fiducial points A-D on the reference film (Fig 5); SAS for means and SDs |
| Skeletal landmarks | ANS (apex); ANS (Harvold): point on the inferior surface of the spine where the bony process is 3 mm thick; PNS: most posterior point of the hard palate at the nasal floor-pterygomaxillary fissure intersection; A point: most posterior point on the anterior maxillary contour |
| Dental landmarks | U6C mesiobuccal cusp of U6; U6A apex of U6; UIE incisal edge of the most prominent central; UIA apex of the central incisor |
| Methods catalogued in Fig 1 (literature, as cited by the paper) | a Broadbent 1937: palatal plane at ANS; b Moore 1959: palatal plane at ANS or at the pterygomaxillary fissure; c Riedel 1974: infratemporal fossa + posterior palate; d Ricketts 1981: palatal plane at ANS; e McNamara 1981: best fit of internal palatal structures; f Luder 1981: anterior border and tip of key ridge |
| Fig 2 (Björk & Skieller 1977, nine boys 4-20 y; literature) | O (orbital-floor apposition) 6.4; R_e (resorptive lowering of the nasal floor) 4.6; S_u 11.2, C 2.5, A 14.6 (not defined in the legend); O : R_e = 1.39 (derived), called "approximately 3 : 2" |

### 10h. Ratios and ranks (derived)

- ANS / PNS vertical descent: implant 2.3; structural 1.7. ANS vs ANS-Harvold on implants: 1.62 vs 1.56 (0.06 apart), "irrespective of how this point was defined".

- Sample: 18/35 = 51 %; 12/18 girls = 67 %; T1 10 ± 0.37 y means about 95 % of subjects between 9.3 and 10.7 y if the figure is an SD.

### 10i. Scaling the bias to other intervals (derived; UNVERIFIED for other ages)

Best-fit masked descent per year at 10-14 y: skeletal 1.08-2.24 / 4 = 0.27-0.56 mm; dental 1.33-1.84 / 4 = 0.33-0.46 mm. The masked quantity is nasal-floor remodelling per unit time, not a fixed fraction of tooth movement, so "30-50 %" must not be applied as a correction to short intervals or to older patients. Over 44-94 days in 16-17-year-olds (the Khaleel 2026 intervals, registry) the un-discounted figure is 0.04-0.12 mm, and remodelling at that age is slower than at the peak.
