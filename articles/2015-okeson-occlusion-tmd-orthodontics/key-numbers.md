## Numbers from Okeson's 2018 interview (same author, cites this paper)

| Number | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 of 5 | Etiologic factors orthodontics can influence (occlusion), so its effect on TMD is minimal from the start | [Int-2018 Q2] |
| 1-2 mm | Discrepancy between stable joint and stable occlusal position in most patients; not shown to correlate significantly with TMD symptoms (his opinion) | [Int-2018 Q3] |
| >3-4 mm | Slides from the stable joint to the stable occlusal position that correlate more with symptoms, mostly in epidemiological studies (he cites Seligman and Pullinger 1991). Bigger slides demand more adaptation, and fewer patients can adapt | [Int-2018 Q3] |
| 15-20 years | Ages over which clicking comes and goes, usually without significant symptoms (he cites Magnusson) | [Int-2018 Q4] |
| 1-2 months | Nightly anterior positioning appliance for painful disc displacement with reduction, then stopped | [Int-2018 Q13] |
