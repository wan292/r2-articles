## Scored modules (Section 4.4)

**Pterygoid muscle trismus after a mandibular advancement device (MAD) for OSA**, ICD-10 R25.2 as printed (Sections 4.4.1, 4.4.2)

| Item | Points |
|---|---|
| History of MAD use for OSA | No = skip to "Section 10" of the questionnaire; Yes = 1 |
| Posterior teeth contact in centric occlusion | Yes = 0; No = 1 |
| **Range** | 0–2 (derived; not printed) |
| **Total 0** | Proceed without concern |
| **Total 1–2** | Treat the trismus first; monitor throughout orthodontics |

Worked example: a 16-year-old with pterygoid muscle trismus after MAD use. The paper says such patients are high risk and must be treated before orthodontics (Section 4.4.1). MAD history alone scores 1, which already means treat first (derived).

**Degenerative joint disease**, questionnaire "Section 4", M26.64 as printed (Section 4.4.3)

| Item | Points |
|---|---|
| Crepitus or grinding with jaw movement | No = skip to "Section 5" (arthralgia); Yes = 1 |
| Degenerative change of condyle or eminence on panoramic or CBCT | No imaging = no points printed (0, derived); imaging without change = 0; with change = 1 |
| Preauricular pain aggravated by function | No = 0; 0–4/10 = 1; 5–10/10 = 2 |
| Occlusal change linked to the joint change | No = 0; Yes = 1 |
| **Range** | 0–5 |
| **0–2** | Proceed, no major concern |
| **2–3** | Proceed, with 6-month follow-up by an orofacial pain specialist |
| **3–5** | Manage the DJD before orthodontics |

- The bands overlap at 2 and at 3 (derived). Scores of 0–1 and 4–5 fall in one band only.
- Crepitus is the gate: without it the module is skipped, so imaging change, pain and occlusal change score nothing here (derived from the branching).
- A "yes" to function pain scores at least 1, even at 0/10 (derived).
- Worked example: a 19-year-old with TMJ osteoarthritis, crepitus, function pain and a posterior open bite, counted as high risk (Section 4.4.3).

## Validation plan (Sections 5.5, 8)

1. Pilot: feasibility, clarity, inter-examiner reliability.
2. Repeat administration for reliability.
3. Criterion validity against full DC/TMD examination and imaging.
4. Prospective multi-centre studies for predictive validity: does baseline risk predict TMD onset or flare during treatment?
5. Later: CBCT/MRI for high-risk cases, AI decision support, training in residency curricula, tracking of medico-legal cases (Sections 8.3, 8.4, 9).
