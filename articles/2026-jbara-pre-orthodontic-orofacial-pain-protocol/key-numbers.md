## Validation plan (Sections 5.5, 8)

1. Pilot: feasibility, clarity, inter-examiner reliability.
2. Repeat administration for reliability.
3. Criterion validity against full DC/TMD examination and imaging.
4. Prospective multi-centre studies for predictive validity: does baseline risk predict TMD onset or flare during treatment?
5. Later: CBCT/MRI for high-risk cases, AI decision support, training in residency curricula, tracking of medico-legal cases (Sections 8.3, 8.4, 9).
