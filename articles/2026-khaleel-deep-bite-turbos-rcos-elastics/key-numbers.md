# Key numbers — Khaleel & Al-Nimri 2026 (deep bite: ABT alone vs ABT+RCOS vs ABT+posterior elastics)

Group labels used throughout: **Group I** = anterior bite turbos (ABT) + lower 0.016×0.022 NiTi reverse-curve-of-Spee (RCOS) wire; **Group II** = ABT + 0.014 NiTi both arches + bilateral posterior box elastics; **Group III** = ABT + 0.014 NiTi both arches only. Every table cell below was checked against the page images (Tables 1–5). Where the text and a table disagree, both values are given and the table is treated as the primary source unless stated otherwise.

Inclusion criteria (Methods, verbatim thresholds):

- Deep overbite: > 40 % overlap of the lower incisor crowns
- Mild anteroposterior skeletal discrepancy: ANB 1°–5° (Group I mean ANB in Table 1 is 5.14 ± 2.16°, so part of that arm sits above the ceiling)
- Intact mandibular dentition, third molars excluded
- Average or reduced lower facial height
- Well-aligned lower incisors or minimal crowding 1–3 mm (Table 1 crowding means 1.25 / 1.58 / 1.81 mm)
- Average smile line

Exclusion criteria:

- Overjet > 6 mm
- Unerupted mandibular second molars
- Previous orthodontic treatment
- Medical condition that could affect tooth eruption

- Point I: no between-group test reported anywhere.

Sign convention note (my read; the paper states none): posterior values are positive and described as extrusion, so positive = occlusal movement for those rows. Lower incisor tip values are negative and described as both "downward" and "intruded". Point I values are positive and described as "true intrusion". The tip and Point I lie on the same tooth, so as printed they carry opposite signs for the same direction of movement. Treat the Table 4 signs as unresolved when quoting; quote magnitudes with the paper's own verbal direction attached.

Text vs table for Group II lower extrusion: abstract "up to 1.1 mm"; Results text "0.6 mm at the premolars to 1.5 mm at the molars"; Table 4 0.90–1.19 mm with the premolars (1.19 / 1.14) larger than the molars (1.12 / 1.08 / 0.93 / 0.90).
