# Assessment of three different techniques in correcting deep overbite: a prospective clinical study (Khaleel & Al-Nimri, Angle Orthod 2026)

Khaleel R, Al-Nimri K. Angle Orthod. 2026;96(5):528-535. DOI 10.2319/100925-850.1. Jordan University of Science and Technology, Irbid.

## Bottom line

Adding a lower 0.016×0.022 NiTi reverse-curve wire or posterior box elastics to anterior bite turbos roughly halved the time to correct a deep bite compared with turbos alone (56 and 44 vs 94 days, Table 2). The RCOS wire paid for its speed with 7.5° of lower incisor flaring and distal molar tipping; elastics paid with the largest mandibular plane opening. Allocation was by operator judgment, so the elastics-over-RCOS ranking is the least secure finding.

## What they did

- Prospective, non-randomised, single operator, JUST postgraduate clinic. 81 enrolled, 80 completed (one lost both turbos; the dropout's group is not stated); three equal groups of 27 as reported (Table 1); mean ages 15.8 / 16.0 / 17.4 y. The printed post-hoc standard errors are consistent with about 29/31/20 analysed, not 27 each, and the turbos-alone outcome column matches the 2022 RCT's turbo arm on 14 of 26 values to two decimals (see critical appraisal), so treat Group III as neither equal-sized nor independent until the authors clarify. The operator assigned groups on lower incisor alignment/inclination and patient cooperation.
- Inclusion: overbite >40%, ANB 1-5°, average or reduced LFH, well-aligned or minimally crowded lower incisors (1-3 mm), average smile line, intact lower arch to the second molars; overjet >6 mm excluded.
- All: 0.022 Roth brackets, ABTs formed in a silicone mould (Mini Mold, Aditek) and bonded with Transbond to the palatal of the upper centrals immediately after bonding, upper 0.014 NiTi. Group I: lower 0.016×0.022 NiTi RCOS cinched distal to the 7s. Group II: 0.014 NiTi both arches + 3/16-inch medium bilateral box elastics from upper premolars/molars to lower premolars/molars. Group III: 0.014 NiTi both arches only.
- Corrected = overbite <30% plus posterior Shimstock contact, reviewed every 14 days.
- Cephs at the start of treatment (T1) and after levelling (T2); mandibular superimposition on the corpus axis at Pm (Ricketts), maxillary on the palatal vault (Broadbent); Point I at 0.66 of incisor length (Greig) as the "true" intrusion marker.

## What they found

- Duration (Table 2): 55.72 / 44.42 / 94.15 d (abstract 94.2), P<.001, every pair significant (I vs II 11.3 d, P=.010). Table 2 prints Group I SD 10.52 and Group III mean 94.15; the Results text says 20.5 and 94.4, and the Tukey mean differences in Table 2 (55.72 + 38.68; 44.42 + 49.98) both back-calculate to 94.4.
- Overbite reduction 3.92 / 3.65 / 3.57 mm, called comparable; no test reported.
- Group I: LI-CA +7.46°, incisor tip −2.46 mm, Point I 0.99 mm (Table 4 prints the tip negative and Point I positive with no sign convention stated; both are described as intrusion), L6/L7 distal tipping 5.99°/8.94°, premolar extrusion ~1.05 mm (Tables 3-4).
- Group II: lower posterior extrusion 0.90-1.19 mm, upper 0.28-0.42 mm (the only group with real upper extrusion), LI-CA +3.46°, Point I 0.71 mm.
- Group III: lower posterior eruption 0.86-1.30 mm, LI-CA +1.55°, incisor tip −1.25 mm, upper incisor −0.43 mm, Point I 0.86 mm.
- Skeletal (Table 5): Mn-Mx +1.35 / +1.84 / +0.69°, FH-Mn +0.97 / +1.31 / +0.32°, LFH +0.97 / +1.56 / +0.41%; all significant within groups; between groups only II vs III differed, for Mn-Mx (P=.011) and FH-Mn (P=.026); LFH showed no between-group difference as printed.

## Why it matters

The literature anchor is the group's 2022 RCT (Al-Zoubi & Al-Nimri): turbos plus a plain lower 0.016×0.022 NiTi took about 3.15 months and RCOS alone 4.85 months (different T1, review interval and endpoint definition, so the match is approximate). Turbos alone again took ~94 d here, so the new finding is that an auxiliary on top of turbos brings levelling to 6-8 weeks. The trade-off is a triangle: speed (elastics) vs incisor position (RCOS proclines 7.5°) vs vertical control (elastics open the mandibular plane most, turbos alone least). My read: Point I moved 0.7-0.9 mm in the two arms with no intrusive mechanics, so the "1 mm true intrusion" credited to the RCOS is not shown to exceed the other groups; no between-group test on Point I is reported.

## One-line caveat

Non-randomised, operator-allocated (partly by cooperation, which drives elastic wear), unblinded operator judging the endpoint, no untreated control, "nongrowing" asserted at mean age 16, no follow-up past the end of levelling, and an apparent undisclosed overlap with the 2022 turbo arm.
