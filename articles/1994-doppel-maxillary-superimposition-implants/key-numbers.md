# Key numbers — Doppel 1994

Doppel DM, Damon WM, Joondeph DR, Little RM. An investigation of maxillary superimposition techniques using metallic implants. Am J Orthod Dentofacial Orthop 1994;105(2):161–168. Every table cell below was read from the page images (Tables I–III, pages 163–164) and checked against the text extraction. Page numbers are the journal pages. "Derived" means computed here from the printed values; nothing derived appears in the paper.

## Method / protocol

| Item | Paper says |
|---|---|
| Registration | T1 and T2 tracings superimposed on the three implants, then evaluated for anatomic best fit |
| Metric | Maximum distance each structure varied from perfect superimposition; for curved surfaces without definite landmarks, several points measured and averaged |
| Tracing convention | Where a structure has an anterior and posterior, or a superior and inferior, surface, the posterior and the inferior surface were traced |
| Structures traced (Methods list) | 1 clinoid process (anterior and posterior aspects); 2 anterior cranial base as the sella–nasion line; 3 ethmoid triad (lesser wings of the sphenoid and orbital roofs bisected by the anterior border of the greater wings); 4 zygomatic process of the maxilla, anterior and posterior aspects; 5 lateral border of the orbits; 6 orbital floors; 7 infraorbital foramen; 8 PTM; 9 palate, superior and inferior aspects; 10 internal structure of the palate; 11 central incisor; 12 first permanent molar; 13 ANS; 14 A point (innermost curvature from ANS to the alveolar crest); 15 PNS; 16 nasion; 17 radiopacities appearing serially |
| Rotation measures | Angle between the ANS–PNS line and the line from the lateral to the anterior implant; angle between SN and the same implant line |
| SN transfer | SN drawn on film 1 and transferred to film 2 after superimposing on cranial-base structures as described by Björk (ref 16: anterior cranial fossa and anterior wall of sella) |
| Anatomic methods illustrated (one case) | Fig 1 palatal plane at ANS; Fig 2 palatal plane at PTM; Fig 3 infratemporal fossa at the posterior palate; Fig 4 best fit of internal palatal structures; Fig 5 anterior zygomatic border at its most inferior point; Fig 6 posterior zygomatic process on the internal palate above the incisors; Fig 7 implants |

## Measurement and error

| Item | Paper says |
|---|---|
| Operator | One, for all tracings |
| Repeat tracings | 10 films traced twice at a 2-week interval: "accuracy of ± 0.25 mm", stated as a total tracing error of 0.5 mm |
| Literature benchmark | Thurow 1951 (cited): tracing accuracy no better than 0.5 mm |
| Pencil | 3H lead in an architect's pencil, needle point maintained throughout |
| Calipers | Digital (Fowler Ultra-Cal II), accurate to 0.01 mm |
| Head-position error (cited) | Julius: the zygomatic implant varied as much as 3 mm with rotational head movement; patients can rotate 3° and tip 5° while fixed in a cephalostat; Masumoto and Damon: significant projection error for landmarks lateral to the sagittal plane |
| Implant-fit error (Discussion) | Some implants may have matched only because of slight head-position change in the transverse plane; closely spaced implants make the apparent error of a structure a function of its distance from the implants; bilateral implants were not available |
| Blinding | None stated; the same operator traced, superimposed and judged fit |
| Not reported | Per-landmark method error, Dahlberg value, inter-examiner check, magnification |
