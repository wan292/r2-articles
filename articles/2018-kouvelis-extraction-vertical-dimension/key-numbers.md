# Key numbers — Kouvelis 2018 (4-premolar extraction and the vertical dimension: systematic review)

Kouvelis G, Dritsas K, Doulis I, Kloukos D, Gkantidis N. Effect of orthodontic treatment with 4 premolar extractions compared with nonextraction treatment on the vertical dimension of the face: a systematic review. Am J Orthod Dentofacial Orthop 2018;154(2):175-87. doi:10.1016/j.ajodo.2018.03.007.

**How the numbers were checked.** Every value in Tables I-III and the flow chart below was read from the page images (journal pp 177, 179-184, 187). The text layer prints "+" as "1" and "±" as "6" (for example "11.5 mm" is +1.5 mm) and drops minus signs, so signs here come from the images. "Derived" means computed here from the printed numbers; the arithmetic is in `checks.py` beside this file. Its inputs are printed values rounded to 0.1, so recomputed P values are approximate, and least reliable when the difference between groups is only 0.1-0.3.

**Sign conventions:**

- **Change:** every Table III value is T1 − T0, posttreatment minus pretreatment, as mean ± SD.
- **Mandibular plane angles** (SN-GoGn, FMA, FH-MP, SN-GoMe, MP-HP, PP-GoMe, PP-GoGn): a positive change means the plane steepened (backward, clockwise rotation, face lengthening); a negative change means it flattened (forward rotation).
- **Linear heights** (N-Me, ANS-Me, Me-PP), in mm: a positive change means the height increased.
- **Between-group difference (derived):** Ex − Nonex. For an angle, a negative difference is the direction the "wedge" theory predicts (less opening, or more closing, with extraction).
- **Naming:** the review prints "Class II, 1" and defines it in the footnotes as "Class II Subdivision 1"; Division 1 is meant. It spells ref 16 "Luppanapornlap" in the text and tables and "Luppanapornlarp" in the reference list; the reference-list spelling is used here. Hosseinzadeh-Nik's two outcomes are printed "Pog-SN7" and "B-SN7" (mm); the reference line is not defined in the review; the text describes them as the vertical position of point B and Pog (p 181).

## Eligibility (p 176)

| Element | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Designs | Any: RCTs, non-randomised or quasi-randomised trials, prospective and retrospective studies with a treated comparison group |
| Participants | Any age, full fixed appliances in both arches |
| Intervention | Fixed appliances with 4 premolar extractions, one per quadrant |
| Comparison | Fixed appliances without extraction |
| Outcome | Vertical dimension of skeletal craniofacial structures, measured on radiographs, before and after treatment |
| Excluded | Animal and in-vitro studies, orthognathic surgery, case reports, studies with fewer than 10 patients |

## Review methods (pp 176-178)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Study selection | Independent and in duplicate (G.K., K.D.), not blinded to authors, institutions or results; disagreements resolved with I.D. and N.G. |
| Data extraction | Independent and in duplicate (G.K., K.D.), customised forms; consensus with the last author |
| Planned effect measure | Mean differences and SDs for continuous outcomes; unit of analysis the patient |
| Missing data | Authors e-mailed. Missing SDs to be derived from t or P values; P printed as 0.000 to be imputed as 0.0004 (worst case). Table III gives within-group changes (means, with SDs where available), not mean differences, so none of this appears in the results |
| Heterogeneity sources named | Growth status; vertical, sagittal skeletal and dental pattern; anchorage; biomechanics (e.g. headgear); teeth extracted; type of control; outcome measure. Sex distribution was ignored "since a differential effect due to sex was not expected" (p 177) |
| Risk of bias | ROBINS-I, 7 domains, two assessors (G.K., K.D.); disagreements resolved with the last author (N.G.) |
| Risk-of-bias rules stated | No study could be rated low on confounding by definition. Studies with "no information" in at least 1 domain were judged "between serious and critical" overall. All disagreements were between serious and critical ratings (p 178) |
| Synthesis | Narrative only: "Clinical heterogeneity was high" (p 178) |

Notes on how the ratings line up (neutral observations, derived):

- Staggers is rated critical overall although no single domain is critical (two NI, one serious); the paper's stated rule for "no information" studies allows "between serious and critical" (p 178). Paquette and Hosseinzadeh-Nik, each with one NI and no serious domain, are rated serious.
- Sex distribution is given as a reason for the confounding rating in Kumari (serious), Sivakumar (critical) and Staggers (no information), while the Methods ignored sex (p 177); Paquette's groups (20 F / 13 M vs 11 F / 19 M) are rated moderate.
- Hosseinzadeh-Nik's groups were defined by different diagnoses (bimaxillary protrusion vs none) and are rated moderate on confounding; Luppanapornlarp is rated critical for different baseline characteristics.

## Derived numbers

All values below are derived from the printed tables; `checks.py` holds the arithmetic.

### a. The sample

- **Patients:** 857 in total, 430 extraction and 427 nonextraction (Table I).
- **Group sizes:** 14 (Hayasaki Class II extraction) to 45 (Staggers nonextraction).
- **Ages:** group means 10.8 (Kirschneck extraction) to 18.5 y (Sivakumar nonextraction); age NA for Luppanapornlarp.
- **Sex not reported:** Kumari and Staggers, 164 patients.
- **Female-only:** 4 studies (Bravo, Hosseinzadeh-Nik, Katsaros, Kirschneck).
- **Largest within-study age gap:** Basciftci Class II/1, 17.4 vs 12.7 y (4.7 y); with the baseline SN-GoGn gap (39.3 vs 34.7°), the reason its confounding is rated critical (Table II).

### b. Baseline and treatment imbalances

- **Baseline angle:** the extraction group had the steeper mandibular plane in 10 of 13 comparisons that give both values (Table I). Ex − Nonex: Basciftci Class I +1.6°, Basciftci Class II +4.6°, Bravo +1.0°, Hayasaki Class I +0.9°, Katsaros +2.3°, Kirschneck +0.9°, Kocadereli +2.2°, Kumari +1.5°, Luppanapornlarp +5.8°, Porto +2.0°. The nonextraction group was steeper in Gkantidis (−0.7°), Hayasaki Class II (−0.7°) and Paquette (−2.0°).
- **Treatment duration:** 8 studies report it by group, and extraction treatment was longer in 7 of them (9 of 10 comparisons). The exception is Sivakumar, 1.5 vs 2.9 y. Longer treatment means more growth in the linear measures.
- **Magnification:** not corrected (NA) in 8 of 14 studies; corrected with the machine factor in 4 and a reference ruler in 2. This affects only the linear measures.
- **Mechanics:** some treatment details are given for 7 studies and none for the other 7 (Table I c). Only Gkantidis gave the two groups opposite vertical mechanics, non-extrusive with extraction and extrusive without, which should have favoured a smaller vertical increase in the extraction group; none was found.

### d. Vote count and direction

- **Tests:** 42 between-group tests in Table III (20 angular, 19 linear, 3 ratios). 4 were significant (9.5 %); about 2.1 would be expected by chance at α 0.05.
  - Angular: 2 of 20 significant (Porto, Paquette), in opposite directions.
  - Linear: 2 of 19 significant (Kumari, Sivakumar), in opposite directions.
- **Direction of the mandibular-plane-angle difference** (one angle per comparison, SN-GoGn where reported): Ex − Nonex was negative (the "wedge" direction) in 11 of 15 comparisons, zero in 1 (Staggers) and positive in 3 (Kirschneck, Sivakumar, Paquette). Apart from Paquette (+2.3°), every difference lies between −1.7° and +0.4°.
- **Within-group growth:** N-Me rose +0.9 to +7.0 mm and ANS-Me +0.6 to +4.0 mm in both arms across studies (Table III). Growth and treatment time dominate the linear changes.

How to read it:

- The pools mix different angles and different reference lines (S-N, FH).
- The extraction groups started steeper in most comparisons (b), and a steeper start can drift back on its own.
- The most favourable subset still excludes an extraction benefit larger than about 1.2° (the lower limit of −1.14° to −1.17°).
- So the defensible statement is "no clinically meaningful effect; if there is one, it is under about 1°", not "exactly zero".

### f. Power (two-sided α 0.05, power 80 %, SD 2.5° for an MP angle)

| Group size | Smallest detectable difference |
|---|---|
| 15 per group | 2.56° |
| 20 per group | 2.21° |
| 30 per group | 1.81° |
| 40 per group | 1.57° |

Single studies of this size could not show differences under about 1.6-2.6°, so each study's "no difference" is not equivalence. The pooled interval (e) is much narrower.
