## Ch 14 (Fig 14.35, headgear line of force vs molar center of resistance)

### Q13 · Promotion 2021

> Figure 14.35 (A). When the bow is above the center of resistance, what is the most likely effect?
>
> - Counterclockwise rotation of the molar
> - Clockwise rotation of the molar

- **Per the paper:** Counterclockwise rotation of the molar (root moves distally, crown tips mesially) when the profile faces right (not stated in the paper) (Proffit, growth-modification chapter, headgear line of force vs molar centre of resistance (Fig 14.35); no topic 8 paper analyses bow height)
- Suppose the distal pull passes above (apical to) the molar's centre of resistance. The root then moves back more than the crown, so the crown tips mesially, which reads as counterclockwise in a right-facing view; check the figure's orientation. Al-Buraiki 2005 (topic 17) bends a long outer bow upward on purpose to get this distal root moment. Brosh only says its force line was near the molar's centre of resistance.
